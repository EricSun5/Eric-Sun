# AGENTS.md

## About Me

I am an Accounting and Management Information Systems student at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. I use this repository to organize my academic work, portfolio projects, analyses, models, and professional development.

## How I Like Things Explained

- Use clear, simple, and natural language.
- Explain technical concepts in plain English before using more advanced terminology.
- Break complicated problems into logical steps.
- When working with calculations, explain what the formula means and how the numbers connect to the answer.
- When reviewing my work, point out specific errors or inconsistencies instead of rewriting everything unnecessarily.
- Keep academic writing professional but natural and easy to understand.
- Do not make assumptions without identifying them.
- Do not invent facts, calculations, sources, citations, or evidence.
- If information is missing or uncertain, clearly identify what still needs to be checked.
- Preserve my voice when helping revise writing.

## Working With My Files

- Read the relevant files before making recommendations.
- Keep existing formatting and organization unless a change is necessary.
- Verify calculations and formulas before suggesting corrections.
- Clearly explain any important changes.
- Do not replace my original analysis or conclusions without explaining why.
- For graded work, help me understand, check, critique, and improve my work rather than completing work I am expected to produce independently.

## Audit and Confidential Information

Never ask me to paste or upload confidential or restricted audit information into an AI assistant.

I should not provide:

- Client names or identifying information when the information is not already public or is protected.
- Confidential client financial information.
- Audit workpapers or internal engagement documentation.
- Personally identifiable information belonging to clients, employees, customers, or other individuals.
- Passwords, login credentials, API keys, access tokens, or security information.
- Nonpublic company records or documents provided under confidentiality restrictions.
- Employer-confidential information.
- Documents or data that I do not have permission to share.
- Licensed course materials or other copyrighted material when sharing them would violate access or use restrictions.

When possible, sensitive examples should be anonymized or replaced with hypothetical information before being used with an AI assistant.

## Repository Conventions

- Organize work by capability and engagement rather than by course, semester, or week.
- Briefs belong in `docs/briefs/`.
- Final recommendations and decision records belong in `docs/decisions/`.
- Sourced data belongs in `data/`.
- Figures and charts belong in `analysis/figures/`.
- Reusable capabilities and models belong in `capabilities/`.
- Do not commit confidential information, credentials, temporary files, or private data.
- Use clear and descriptive filenames.
- Use meaningful Git commit messages that describe what changed.

## AI Use and Prompt Log

For meaningful AI-assisted work, maintain `prompt-log.md` with a record of:

- What I asked the AI to help with.
- What the AI produced.
- Any mistakes, limitations, or questionable outputs.
- What I independently checked, changed, or rejected.

AI-generated work should always be reviewed before it is committed or submitted.
